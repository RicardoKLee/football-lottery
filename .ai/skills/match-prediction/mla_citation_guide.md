# MLA 第九版学术引用规范与范例指南 (MLA Citation Guide)

本指南作为 `match-prediction` Skill 的报告标准参考手册，规定了分析报告末尾引用专业数据源时必须遵循的 **MLA (Modern Language Association) 第九版** 学术规范。

---

## 1. 网站及网页通用引用格式
```text
Author/Organization. "Title of the Article/Webpage." Title of the Website, Date of Publication/Last Update, URL. Accessed Day Month Year.
```

---

## 2. 经典数据源引用范例

### (1) 国际足联 / 各大联赛官网数据
```text
FIFA. "FIFA/Coca-Cola World Ranking Technical Movement." FIFA.com, FIFA, 2024, www.fifa.com/fifaplus/en/articles/technical-movement-of-the-fifa-coca-cola-world-ranking. Accessed 20 Jun. 2026.
```

### (2) Transfermarkt 转会市场身价与伤病数据
```text
Transfermarkt. "Spain National Team - Squad, Market Values and Injuries." Transfermarkt.com, Transfermarkt, May 2026, www.transfermarkt.com/spain/startseite/verein/3377. Accessed 20 Jun. 2026.
```

### (3) ELO 理论积分数据
```text
Elo Ratings. "World Football Elo Ratings: Live Rankings." EloRatings.net, Jun. 2026, www.eloratings.net/World.tsv. Accessed 20 Jun. 2026.
```

### (4) WhoScored / FBref 战术与高阶数据
```text
FBref. "Match Report: Real Madrid vs Manchester City." FBref.com, Sports Reference, May 2026, www.fbref.com/en/matches/2026. Accessed 20 Jun. 2026.
```
